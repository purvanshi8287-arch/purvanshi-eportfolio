India’s corporate debt market, five years in view.

A review of market size and growth, primary-market mobilisation, fresh issuance, secondary trading and FPI participation from FY2021–22 to FY2025–26, using SEBI and RBI publications alongside NSE and BSE market information.

Findings with boundaries

The report compares five years of market data and discusses the dominance of private placements. FY26 figures are provisional or approximate. FPI net flows cover all debt, not corporate debt alone, and the available rating series combines AAA with AA+ and above, so an AAA-only share is not established. Source-series checks are noted in the workbook.

Purpose: understand changes in the Indian corporate debt market. Process: compare exchange, regulator and central-bank data in an Excel workbook, then interpret the patterns in a Word report. Outcome: a documented five-year review; no live investment recommendation is claimed.
